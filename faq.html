When and where do I pick up?

, in . Choose a pickup window when you order. We’ll send the exact pickup spot in your confirmation email.

When do orders close?

Orders close Wednesday at 8pm for that Saturday, because we start mixing dough on Thursday. Late orders roll over to the following Saturday unless we have extra to spare.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, GCash or bank transfer. For GCash and bank transfer, our details come with your confirmation email; please pay by Friday 6pm and reply with a screenshot. Cash is paid at pickup.

Is my order confirmed once I send the email?

Not yet. We reply by Thursday to confirm your order and total. If you haven’t heard from us by Thursday evening, please email again.

What about allergies?

Each bake’s allergens are listed on the menu. If you have a severe allergy, email us before ordering.

Can I place a large or custom order?

Yes. For orders of 10 or more items, or for parties and events, email us at least one week ahead. Large orders need a 50% deposit by GCash or bank transfer to secure your slot.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Changes and cancellations are free until Wednesday 8pm. After that the dough is already made for you, so prepaid orders can’t be refunded, but we’re happy to move your order to the next Saturday if you tell us by Friday noon.

What if I can’t make my pickup?

Let us know as early as you can and send someone in your place if needed. Unclaimed orders are held until noon on Saturday. After that they can’t be refunded and are shared with the community.

How should I store my bread?

Keep sourdough cut-side down on a board or in a paper bag for two to three days, and freeze slices for longer. Focaccia is best the same day; warm it in the oven to revive it. Cinnamon rolls warm up nicely in 10 seconds in the microwave.
