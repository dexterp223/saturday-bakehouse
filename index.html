About the bakehouse

Your neighborhood bakery in Nuvali

Saturday Bakehouse started as a weekend habit: real sourdough, baked slowly, shared with neighbors who kept asking for another loaf. Now we bake one morning a week for Nuvali, in small batches, so every order is fresh from the oven.
