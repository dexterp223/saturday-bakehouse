Our story

Baked in Nuvali, one Saturday at a time

Saturday Bakehouse began in a home kitchen with a jar of sourdough starter and a simple wish: bread like the kind you find in small bakeries abroad, made right here in Laguna. The first loaves went to family and neighbors. They came back asking for more, then their friends started asking too.

So we made it a weekly tradition. Every Saturday we bake a small batch for our community: crusty sourdough for breakfast, focaccia for the family table, and cinnamon rolls for anyone who deserves a treat after a long week.

How we bake

Dough is mixed on Thursday and rests slowly through Friday, which builds flavor and a better crust. Everything goes into the oven before dawn on Saturday, so what you pick up is hours old, not days.

We keep it simple: flour, water, salt, real butter and time. No preservatives and no shortcuts, which is why we bake only what’s been ordered plus a few extra.

Why order ahead

Baking to order means nothing goes to waste and your bread is set aside with your name on it. Reserve by Wednesday night, pick up Saturday morning in Nuvali, and start your weekend with something fresh.
